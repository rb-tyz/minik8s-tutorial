Stage 3.4：按前缀列举

让 KV 的 list 根据请求里的 prefix 筛选键。测试器写入 pods/ 和 nodes/ 两组键，再只查询 pods/。

测试方式

entries 恰好是 pods/a、pods/b，不含 nodes/n。

POST /call  {"op":"put","key":"pods/a","value":"a"}
POST /call  {"op":"put","key":"pods/b","value":"b"}
POST /call  {"op":"put","key":"nodes/n","value":"n"}
POST /call  {"op":"list","prefix":"pods/"}

实现思路

用 strings.HasPrefix 等简单匹配；不要把 prefix 当作完整键。
