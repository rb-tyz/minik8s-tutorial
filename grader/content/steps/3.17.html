Stage 3.17：删除事件保留旧值

删除已订阅的键时，给订阅者留下 DELETE 事件。测试器用 next 检查删除前的值和这次删除的版本。

测试方式

DELETE 事件的 value=full，revision 大于此前的 PUT revision。

# 版本数字为示例，测试器使用前一步响应中的实际版本
POST /call  {"op":"list","prefix":"delete-event/"}
POST /call  {"op":"watch","watch":"del","prefix":"delete-event/","revision":0}
POST /call  {"op":"put","key":"delete-event/a","value":"full"}
POST /call  {"op":"next","watch":"del","timeoutMs":500}
POST /call  {"op":"delete","key":"delete-event/a"}
POST /call  {"op":"next","watch":"del","timeoutMs":500}

实现思路

在移除当前值前保留旧 entry，用删除提交分配新 revision。
